## 1.6 Advantages and Limitations of Artificial Intelligence

**Advantages:**
- Automation and operational efficiency.
- Processing large volumes of data.
- Improved consistency and decision support.
- Ability to identify complex patterns.

**Limitations:**
- Dependence on data quality and availability.
- Computational and infrastructure requirements.
- Bias, reliability and ethical concerns.
- Limited contextual understanding in certain applications.
